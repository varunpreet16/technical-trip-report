01 / OUR JOURNEY A learning experience

01 — THE BEGINNING

Introduction

The technical trip was organized to provide students with an opportunity to experience learning outside the traditional classroom environment.

The educational technical trip to TechNova Solutions was organized to give BSIT students an opportunity to explore the professional world of information technology. The visit aimed to introduce students to the actual working environment of IT professionals and provide a better understanding of how technology is used in business and software development.

During the visit, students were introduced to different areas of the company, including software development, web development, database management, and quality assurance. The experience provided valuable insights into the skills, tools, and teamwork required in the IT industry.

04 — THE EXPERIENCE

Activities & Observations

A closer look at the activities and experiences throughout the trip.

01

FIRST ACTIVITY

Company Orientation

The visit started with an introduction to the company, its services, and its role in the technology industry. The students learned about different IT career opportunities and the responsibilities of employees.

02

SECOND ACTIVITY

Software Development Demonstration

Students observed how developers create and maintain software applications. The discussion covered programming languages, development environments, debugging, and teamwork.

03

THIRD ACTIVITY

Database and System Management

The company introduced basic database management and explained how information is stored, organized, and protected. Students learned why database security and proper data management are important.

04

FOURTH ACTIVITY

Software Testing and Quality Assurance

Students learned about the importance of testing software applications to ensure they function correctly and meet user requirements. The discussion covered different testing methods, tools, and the role of quality assurance in the development process.

05 — WHAT WE DISCOVERED

Knowledge & Learnings

Valuable lessons and insights gained from the experience.

The technical trip helped us understand that working in the IT industry requires more than just knowing how to program. IT professionals must also have problem-solving skills, creativity, communication skills, and the ability to work with a team.

As IT students, we also realized that the knowledge used in software development, database management, and quality assurance can be applied when creating games. The visit gave us a better idea of what to expect in our future careers and encouraged us to continue improving our technical skills.

Overall, the experience helped us appreciate the value of practical learning and showed us that education extends beyond textbooks and classroom discussions.

06 — LOOKING BACK

Conclusion

The technical trip was a meaningful and educational experience that allowed us to gain knowledge through actual observation and participation. It provided students with a better understanding of how technology companies operate and how different IT professionals contribute to software development.

The activities and discussions helped connect classroom lessons with real-world applications. This experience also encouraged students to become more prepared for future employment and professional responsibilities in the IT industry.

07 — MOVING FORWARD

Recommendations

Future technical trips should continue to provide students with opportunities to explore actual workplaces and learn directly from professionals.

It is recommended that students be given more hands-on activities and opportunities to interact with IT professionals. Demonstrations of actual software development projects, game development processes, and database systems would also make the experience more engaging and informative.
